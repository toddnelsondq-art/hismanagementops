DQ OPS guide

Welcome to DQ OPS

Use this guide for a quick explanation of each area. What you can see and change depends on your role and assigned locations, so some options described here may not appear on your account.

Getting around

Use the menu on the left on computers, phones, and tablets. On a smaller screen it appears as a compact icon rail; select the menu button to expand the labels. Select your location near the top of a page whenever a location selector is available.

Your role controls which menu choices and locations are visible.

Back to contents

Dashboard

Shows a quick summary of daily operations, maintenance, FPC work, Incident Reports, inspections, upcoming visits, and location performance. Use the time-period and location selectors to narrow the information. Area Managers and above can select Customize dashboard to show or hide widgets, change their order, and save a default time period and location. Reset to default restores the standard layout.

Back to contents

Task Lists

Open the correct checklist, complete each assigned item, and attach a photo when requested. Prep-quantity items allow a number instead of a simple checkmark. Finish the list only after required work is complete.

Back to contents

Temp Logs

Select the correct temperature list and session, then record each required food or equipment temperature. Readings outside the expected range should be corrected promptly and documented according to store procedures.

Back to contents

Maintenance

Create and follow work orders, review preventive-maintenance tasks, and find equipment or vendor information. Include a useful description, priority, assignment, target date, and photo whenever possible. Update the status as work progresses.

Back to contents

Location Health

Area Managers and above can review assigned UniFi Protect cameras, connection status, and current snapshots. Directors and Owners assign cameras to DQ OPS locations. Select Refresh cameras to retrieve the latest status and images.

DQ OPS does not store recordings. Use UniFi Protect for live video, playback, and exporting clips.

Back to contents

Maintenance Schedule & Daily Log

Maintenance team members can enter a planned work schedule and later update the same day with actual start and end time, unpaid break minutes, locations, accomplishments, and follow-up notes. Worked hours are calculated automatically. The Director of Operations and Owner can review full entries and decide which Area Managers may see hours-only summaries.

Back to contents

Resources

This is the starting point for FPC, Documents, Training, and Reports. It also contains useful company links. Managers can add links and control the minimum role and location that can see them.

Back to contents

FPC

Stores facility-performance inspection files and the repair or correction items created from them. Select a location, open the appropriate inspection, and update each item’s assignment, priority, target date, comments, photos, and status.

Back to contents

Documents

Provides location-specific store documents and shared files. Choose a location to filter the list. Area Managers and above can upload a file or add a shared link with a title, category, and notes.

Back to contents

Training

Contains food-safety practice quizzes and other training tools. Choose the employee or manager track and the available quiz length. Practice results are for study and are not official ServSafe exam results.

Back to contents

Reports

Review completed checklist and temperature-log history. Filter by the locations you are allowed to view, open a completed day for details, and use the available export tools when records need to be saved or shared.

Back to contents

Smallwares

Managers can request replacement or additional smallwares for their store. Enter the item, quantity, and reason. Area Managers and above can review requests and mark them approved or declined.

Back to contents

Incident Reports

Shift Managers and above can report cash shortages, deposit or register discrepancies, employee concerns, customer incidents, safety issues, and other matters needing management attention. Enter the facts and immediate action taken. Store Managers review Shift Manager reports; Area Managers and above review Store Manager reports. Authorized supervisors can document follow-up and mark a report resolved.

This area does not replace calling emergency services or immediately contacting a supervisor during an active emergency.

Back to contents

Calendar

Shows scheduled visits, FPC follow-ups, inspections, meetings, and important events. Area Managers and above can add events for their assigned locations and arrange upcoming priorities.

Back to contents

Notices

Select Notices at the top of the app to read important communications. The badge shows unread items. Managers can post a notice from Manage and choose the roles that should receive it.

Back to contents

Manage

Contains manager-only setup tools. Use it to add a one-day task, maintain permanent checklist items and schedules, post notices, create alert rules, review notification attempts, manage tablets, and manage users. Area Managers and above can also push a store-tablet alarm when required cleaning or temperature work is incomplete. DQ OPS records who sent and acknowledged it, resolves it when the work is completed, and texts the store manager if it is not acknowledged in time.

Changes to permanent checklists affect future days. Check the editing scope before saving.

Back to contents

Locations

Shows the address and phone number for each location you can access. Directors and Owners can update location contact information; other authorized managers can use it as a directory.

Back to contents

Receipts

Area Managers and above can upload purchase receipts by location, vendor, date, amount, and category. Use the location and date filters to find prior receipts or download the receipt list.

Back to contents

Store inspections

Area Managers and above can complete the store-visit, food-safety, or cleanliness inspection lists. Score each line, add comments or optional photos, and save the visit. Use the chart filters to review individual scores and rolling performance.

Back to contents

Users, tablets, and alerts

Add each person with the correct role and assigned locations. Employees use a four-digit PIN on a tablet locked to their store; managers use email and password. Alert rules can monitor overdue checklists or temperature logs and send email or opted-in text notifications.

Only assign access a person needs. Text messages require the user’s separate voluntary SMS opt-in.

Open the SMS opt-in form. Each person must enter the same mobile number saved in their HIS OPS user profile, check the consent box, and save their preference.

Back to contents
