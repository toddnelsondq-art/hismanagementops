SMS Consent

Last updated: July 18, 2026

HIS OPS is an internal operations application used by authorized restaurant employees, managers, area managers, maintenance personnel, directors, and ownership. Users may receive SMS/text messages only after they are added as an authorized user and their phone number is entered into their HIS OPS user profile by management or through an approved user import/onboarding process.

How Users Consent

Users consent to receive SMS/text messages when they provide their mobile phone number to company management for HIS OPS operational alerts and agree to receive text messages related to store operations, checklist completion, temperature logs, maintenance assignments, PM tasks, FPC repair items, calendar notices, and weekly assignment summaries.

Consent language used for HIS OPS users:

By providing my mobile phone number for HIS OPS, I agree to receive operational text messages from Average Guys Business Services / HIS OPS. Messages may include checklist reminders, temperature log reminders, maintenance work order assignments, PM task reminders, FPC repair assignments, calendar notices, store notices, and weekly assignment summaries. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. I understand that my mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-Out and Help

  • Users can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text message.
  • Users can request help by replying HELP.
  • Users may also contact company management to update or remove their phone number from HIS OPS.

Sample Messages

  • HIS OPS work order assigned. Location: Roseville. Task: Repair freezer door gasket. Sign in for details. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • HIS OPS alert: Spicer closing checklist is overdue. Sign in for details. Reply HELP for help.
  • HIS OPS weekly assignments: 3 open item(s). Sign in for details. Reply STOP to opt out.
