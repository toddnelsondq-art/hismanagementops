SMS Preferences and Consent

Last updated: August 7, 2026

HIS OPS is an internal operations application operated by Average Guys Business Services for authorized restaurant employees, managers, area managers, maintenance personnel, directors, and ownership.

How to Opt In

Authorized HIS OPS users may voluntarily opt in by completing the public form below. Entering a phone number in an employee profile does not, by itself, provide SMS consent. The user must personally submit this form with the consent checkbox selected.

SMS consent is optional. It is not a condition of employment, access to HIS OPS, or receipt of non-SMS operational communications. Users can submit the form without selecting the checkbox to record that they do not consent.

Set Your SMS Preference

The checkbox is intentionally unchecked. If you leave it unchecked and select “Save SMS preference,” your preference will be recorded as declined.

By saving your preference, you acknowledge the SMS Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

What Happens Next

  • If the checkbox is selected, the number is recorded as opted in for HIS OPS operational messages.
  • If the checkbox is not selected, the number is recorded as not opted in and will not receive HIS OPS operational texts.
  • Users may change their preference by submitting this form again with the same mobile number.
  • Users can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Sample Messages

  • HIS OPS work order assigned. Location: Roseville. Task: Repair freezer door gasket. Sign in for details. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • HIS OPS alert: Spicer closing checklist is overdue. Sign in for details. Reply HELP for help.
  • HIS OPS weekly assignments: 3 open items. Sign in for details. Reply STOP to opt out.
